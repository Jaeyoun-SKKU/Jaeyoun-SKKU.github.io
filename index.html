About Us

Ryu Lab is based in the College of Medicine at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU). We investigate cellular heterogeneity and immune dynamics using single-cell and spatial multi-omics approaches, integrating transcriptomic, epigenomic, and immune-repertoire data to understand disease mechanisms at single-cell resolution.

Research Focus

scRNA-seq

Single-cell transcriptomic profiling to resolve cellular heterogeneity and states.

CITE-seq

Simultaneous protein and transcriptome measurement at single-cell level.

scATAC-seq

Chromatin accessibility profiling to link regulatory landscapes to cell identity.

scTCR-seq

T-cell receptor repertoire analysis for clonality and antigen-specific responses.

Spatial Transcriptomics

Mapping gene expression within tissue architecture to study the microenvironment.
