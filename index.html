Welcome to Ryu Lab

We are a research group in the College of Medicine at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), dedicated to advancing single-cell and spatial multi-omics approaches to understand immune cell heterogeneity, T-cell biology, and the tumor microenvironment.
